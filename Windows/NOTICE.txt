Codex Wallpaper — independent open-source utility

This application was independently implemented in Swift/AppKit/SwiftUI.
Its loopback CDP approach was informed by the ReTheme project:
https://github.com/duxweb/ReTheme

installed Codex 26.1007.71326. No ReTheme branding, service keys or theme packages
